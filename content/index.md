## Welcome, Vagabonds

You hold in your hands a record of the world as it is known—fragmented, imperfect, and hard-won. Those who walk beneath the banner of **Horizon’s Wake** are not bound by birthplace or blood, but by resolve. Strength is proven. Survival is earned. Only those who endure may call themselves *Vagabonds*.

What follows is not a complete truth, nor is it neutral. It is a collection of places mapped, peoples named, and stories recorded by those who came before you—and by those still living them. Some entries reflect common knowledge. Others are rumor, half-truth, or deliberate omission. What is missing may yet be discovered.

As you read, follow names and places where they lead. Deeper paths branch outward from every page, and the margins are often as revealing as the text itself. When in doubt, return here—or trace your way back through the world—to find your bearings again.

The road ahead is unwritten. This record merely marks where others have walked before you.
